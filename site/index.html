Who came and went

    A rule you can read in a minute.

    No one picks the seven by hand. Code checks every member and every candidate each half hour, and the same rule moves the paper books further down this page.

    To get a slot

    • At least 0.5 ETH traded on its Pons curve in 24 hours
    • Launched more than 6 hours ago
    • Still on its curve, not graduated to Uniswap
    • Rug risk under 30 of 100, with no high flag in the scan
    • Curve fee plus creator tax of at most 5% per trade

    To lose it

    • It graduates off its Pons curve
    • It fails the rug scan on a later check
    • Its 24 hour volume falls under 0.2 ETH
    • The busiest token that passes takes the free slot

    How a buy is split

    • Shares follow the square root of 24 hour volume, so busy tokens get more without swallowing the buy
    • No token takes more than 25%
    • Buys under 0.0002 ETH are skipped

    Does rotating help? Watch.

    Three paper books started with the same ETH. If the rotated quiver cannot beat the one that never rotates, or the one that just buys the busiest token, you will see it here.

    What Chiron is not.

    Read these before you sign anything.

    Not custody, not a fund.Chiron has no key to your wallet and pools nothing. It builds transactions, and your wallet signs or refuses each one.

    Not a promise.Memecoins can go to zero, all seven at once. The rug scan catches common traps, not every one.

    Every trade pays the curve.Pons takes 1% and each token's creator takes a tax on every buy and every sale. Buying and selling straight back loses several percent.

    Seven signatures, not one.Each token is its own transaction, so a full buy pays gas seven times.

    Pons curve tokens only.A token that graduates to Uniswap leaves the quiver and cannot be sold from here.

    Not advice.Not affiliated with Robinhood, Pons or Uniswap.
