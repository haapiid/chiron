01 · The seven

Who are the seven?

Seven slots. A token holds one while it stays on its Pons curve, passes the rug scan and keeps trading. The longer the arrow, the more of each buy it takes.

In and out

    02 · The rule

    Read it in a minute.

    Nobody picks the seven by hand. Code checks every member and every candidate each half hour, and the same rule runs the paper books below.

    Getting in

    Busy, tested, cheap enough

    • At least 0.5 ETH traded on its curve in 24 hours
    • Launched more than 6 hours ago
    • Rug risk under 30 of 100, no high flag
    • Fees of at most 5% per trade
    Staying in

    Out when it stops passing

    • It graduates off its Pons curve
    • It fails a later rug scan
    • Its volume drops under 0.2 ETH a day
    • The busiest token that passes takes the slot
    Sharing a buy

    Busier tokens get more

    • Shares follow the square root of volume
    • No token takes more than 25%
    • Buys under 0.0002 ETH are skipped
    • 10% of every buy goes into $CHIRON, Chiron's own token, as a step you sign
    1. Sign inOne signed message proves the wallet is yours. It sends nothing and costs no gas.
    2. BuyName an amount of ETH. Your wallet signs one buy per token.
    3. RotateWhen a token leaves, sell it and move that ETH to your lightest slots.
    4. SellOne plan sells all seven back to ETH, whenever you choose.

    03 · The record

    Does rotating earn its keep?

    Three paper books, same ETH, same start. If the rotated seven cannot beat the seven left alone, you will see it here.

    04 · Honest limits

    What it is not.

    Read these before you sign anything.

    Not custody.Chiron holds no key and pools nothing. It builds transactions; your wallet signs or refuses each one.

    Not a promise.Memecoins can go to zero, all seven at once. The rug scan catches common traps, not every one.

    Not free.Pons takes 1% and each creator takes a tax on every buy and sale. Buying and selling straight back loses several percent.

    Not one click.Each token is its own transaction, so a full draw is seven signatures and seven gas fees.

    Pons curves only.A token that graduates to Uniswap leaves the seven and cannot be sold from here.

    Not advice.Not affiliated with Robinhood, Pons or Uniswap.
